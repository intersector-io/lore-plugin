## Intent

First-time buyers abandon checkout at 58%, mostly between the cart and the
payment step. We want a checkout experience that a new customer can finish
without hesitation, on mobile as well as desktop, without prescribing how the
flow is restructured.

## Success Criteria

- Checkout abandonment for first-time buyers drops below 40%.
- Median time from cart to confirmed order is under 90 seconds.
- Support tickets mentioning "checkout" fall by half within one quarter.
